# Music

See what's playing in Spotify or Apple Music without leaving what you're doing, and control playback from the notch.

## Set up

1. Open **Setup** and choose **Connect Spotify** or **Connect Apple Music**.
2. Allow Music to see and control what’s playing.
3. When macOS asks, allow NotchNotch to control the player.

Choose **Always Allow** to reconnect automatically next time NotchNotch starts.

## Use it

- Hover over the notch or click it to open the player.
- Play, pause, skip, seek, change volume, shuffle and repeat.
- A short announcement appears when a new track starts.

NotchNotch never opens a player on its own. If controls stop working, check **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation** and reconnect from **Setup**.
