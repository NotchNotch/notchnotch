# Music

See what's playing on your Mac without leaving what you're doing, and control playback from the notch: Spotify, Apple Music, your browser and any other app in macOS's Now Playing.

## Set up

1. Open **Setup** and choose **Connect**.
2. Allow Music to see and control what’s playing.

If NotchNotch can't read Now Playing on your Mac, Setup shows **Connect Spotify** and **Connect Apple Music** instead, and macOS asks before NotchNotch controls each.

Choose **Always Allow** to reconnect automatically next time NotchNotch starts.

## Use it

- Hover over the notch or click it to open the player.
- Play, pause, skip and seek. With Spotify and Apple Music connected one by one, also volume, shuffle and repeat.
- A short announcement appears when a new track starts.

NotchNotch never opens a player on its own. If you connected players one by one and controls stop working, check **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation** and reconnect from **Setup**.
